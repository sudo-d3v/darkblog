About Black Cipher

In the hidden layers of the darknet exists a group known as Black Cipher. Its members are masters of encryption, data breaches, and cyber-espionage. Operating in absolute secrecy, they leave behind cryptic traces designed to mislead anyone daring enough to pursue them.

Their presence is whispered about in secured forums and encrypted channels. At the heart of their operations lies a mysterious bot — a digital mouthpiece that delivers instructions, taunts investigators, and drops riddles designed to draw intruders deeper into their trap.

⚠️ This blog is part of a cybersecurity Capture The Flag (CTF) challenge. It is fictional and not affiliated with any real organization.
